About

Discovered by Edward Lorenz in 1963 as a simplified model of atmospheric convection. Three coupled, deterministic equations produce a trajectory that never repeats and never settles — the signature of chaos. 

dx/dt = σ(y − x)
dy/dt = x(ρ − z) − y
dz/dt = xy − βz
