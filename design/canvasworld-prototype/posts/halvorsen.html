Halvorsen and the cost of symmetry

Apr 2026 · 5 min read

Halvorsen’s system is cyclically symmetric — each equation is a rotation of the last — yet the trajectory refuses to look like a tidy kaleidoscope. Symmetry in the ODEs does not imply a symmetric silhouette on screen.

The cyclic form

dx/dt = −a x − 4 y − 4 z − y²
dy/dt = −a y − 4 z − 4 x − z²
dz/dt = −a z − 4 x − 4 y − x²

Parameter a is the damper. Too small and the orbit escapes; too large and it collapses toward the origin. The gallery default sits where the attractor stays bounded and visually dense — usually near 1.4.

Framing cost

Because the three axes trade roles, a single camera angle can hide structure that another angle reveals. The mini player slowly yaws so the asymmetry stays honest instead of freezing a flattering projection.
