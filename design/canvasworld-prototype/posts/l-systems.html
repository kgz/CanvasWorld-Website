L-systems: a grammar for trees

May 2026 · 5 min read

Rewrite a string a dozen times, hand it to a turtle, and a branching structure falls out — no hand-placed geometry, just substitution rules and a stack.

Axiom and rules

axiom: X
X → F+[[X]-X]-F[-FX]+X
F → FF

F steps forward, +/- yaw, and brackets push/pop turtle state. Each generation doubles branch density; by generation 5 the string is long enough that step length must shrink to stay on screen.

Why it belongs here

Attractors grow by integration. L-systems grow by rewriting. Same lab notebook, different engine — both produce structure from a short specification, which is the product thesis in miniature.
