Every visualization ships with sliders before it ships with anything else. The side panel is not chrome for chrome’s sake — it is the product: change a constant, re-seed the trajectory, watch the structure respond.

<VizEmbed slug="lorenz_attractor" label="Lorenz" animateN />

## Why params first

A static render of Lorenz is a poster. A live ρ slider is a laboratory. Shipping params early forced the chrome layout: floating left panel, transport below, stage full-bleed — everything else subordinates to “can I change the system?”

## Copy that stays scientific

Labels keep the Greek letter and the Latin name. Values stay mono and tabular. We avoid marketing adjectives next to the controls; the trajectory is the copy.
